============================================================
1. PRODUCT IDENTITY
============================================================

Treat the following as the canonical product concept:

ContentX is an AI-native World & Content Intelligence Engine.

Its purpose is to allow AI systems to:

1. represent worlds and entities
2. model populations of people
3. generate/sample realistic characters
4. resolve characters into runtime agents
5. simulate behavior
6. evaluate behavior and outcomes
7. transform the resulting structured world/behavior into
   multiple content projections

Core lifecycle:

WORLD
↓
POPULATION
↓
CHARACTER
↓
SNAPSHOT
↓
AGENT
↓
SIMULATION
↓
BEHAVIOR
↓
EVALUATION
↓
CONTENT

The system should communicate this lifecycle consistently.
